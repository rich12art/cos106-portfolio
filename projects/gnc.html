Website mock-up

GNC Computers Hub

GNC Computers Hub is a Lagos shop that sells original UK-used and brand-new laptops. I designed a website mock-up so customers can see the machines, check prices and contact the shop without having to hunt through social media posts.

The homepage uses a clear hero, a product grid, service notes such as a 30-day return policy, and a WhatsApp button. The aim was to make the shop feel easy to trust and easy to reach.
