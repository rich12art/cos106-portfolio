Website mock-up

Tifa Cakes and More

Tifa Cakes and More is a Lagos bakery. I designed a homepage mock-up that introduces the brand, points to the menu and event orders, and sends customers to WhatsApp when they are ready to buy.

The page uses a deep green and cream colour scheme so the bakery feels warm and easy to recognise.
